Our Story

Artbonz Technologies is an Information Technology company dedicated to providing quality digital solutions, professional IT training, and career opportunities for students, businesses, and individuals.

We believe technology is a powerful tool for transformation. Our goal is to bridge the gap between technology education and real-world application by equipping learners with practical skills required in today's digital economy.

Through our learning programs and technology services, we help students develop confidence, businesses improve efficiency, and communities access modern digital solutions.

Frequently Asked Questions

Find answers to the most common questions about our IT training programs, technology services, and bookings.

What courses do you offer?

We offer Computer Packages, Web Development, Graphic Design, Computer Networking, Cyber Security, Data Analysis, Digital Marketing, and other practical IT courses designed for beginners and professionals.

Do I need any previous computer experience?

No. Most of our courses are beginner-friendly. Our instructors guide learners step by step until they become confident in using technology.

How long does each course take?

Course duration depends on the program. Most courses take between 1 and 3 months, with flexible weekday, weekend, and evening classes.

Do you provide certificates after completion?

Yes. Students who successfully complete their training receive a certificate of completion recognizing the skills they have acquired.

Can I learn online?

Yes. Depending on the course, we offer both physical and online learning options, allowing students to study from anywhere.

Do you build websites for businesses?

Yes. We design and develop responsive websites, e-commerce platforms, school systems, business websites, portfolios, and custom web applications tailored to client needs.

How can I book a training session?

Simply click the Book a Session button, complete the booking form, and our team will contact you to confirm your preferred date and time.

How can I contact Artbonz Technologies?

You can reach us through our Contact Form, WhatsApp, phone call, email, or visit our office during working hours. We are always ready to help.
